Questions
Website FAQs

  

    
How long does a website take?
It depends on the number of pages and how ready your content is. A one-page site or landing page is much quicker than a multi-page company website. I'll give you a clear timeline after our first call.

    
Do I need to write all the content myself?
No. I can write or edit the copy based on a conversation about your business, and prepare graphics and imagery. You check it before anything goes live.

    
Can I update the website myself?
Yes, if you want to. I can set up simple editing and show you how. Many clients prefer to send changes to me instead, which is what website support is for.

    
Is website support included, and do I have to take it?
No, it is optional. Support and maintenance is a separate monthly plan with a recurring fee, agreed upfront. Many clients add it at launch so updates, backups and security are handled for them, but your website works and stays yours without it.

    
Can you help with my domain and hosting?
Yes. I can recommend hosting, set up or move your domain, configure business email records and make sure everything renews on time.

    
Can you redesign my existing site?
Yes. I'll review what you have, keep what works, and plan redirects so you don't lose the search visibility you already have.
