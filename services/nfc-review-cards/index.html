  1. Sign in to your Google Business Profile.

  3. Copy the link shown, usually https://g.page/r/…/review, and paste it here.

Questions

Review card and stand FAQs

Is my QR code unique to my business?

Yes. Each table stand gets its own QR code, generated from the Google review link you supply. Before an order can be sent, the code is scanned back to confirm it opens exactly that link.

Where do I get my Google review link?

From your Google Business Profile: choose "Ask for reviews" and copy the link. If you can't find it, send your business name and I'll help you locate it.

Do customers need an app?

No. The QR code works with the normal phone camera, and most modern phones read NFC cards without an extra app.

Will I see a proof before printing?

Yes. You'll receive a proof of your design with your QR code before anything is produced.
