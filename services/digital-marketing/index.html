Questions

Digital marketing FAQs

Can I choose just one service?

Yes. You can start with a single service, such as social media management or your Google Business Profile, and add more later.

Do you guarantee results?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. Results depend on your market, your offer and, for advertising, your budget. What I guarantee is consistent, professional work, honest reporting and practical recommendations.

Is the advertising budget included in your fee?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta from your own account, so you always see and control it. My fee covers planning, setup, creative, monitoring and reporting.

How involved do I need to be?

You approve the monthly plan and each batch of content, answer customer messages and share news or photos when you have them. The rest is handled for you.

Do you work with businesses outside Europe?

Yes. I work with clients across Europe and worldwide. Everything runs online, with calls and reviews scheduled to suit your time zone.
