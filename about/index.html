Who we are

A small studio that takes the details seriously

Gilmour Stellini is a website development and digital marketing studio for small and growing businesses. Behind it is Gilmour himself, who spent more than five years in IT operations as an incident and NOC engineer, keeping production systems monitored, stable and running. Before moving into IT, he worked in the energy industry in England.

That background shapes how we work today. We check things properly, we plan for what can go wrong, and we explain what we are doing in plain language. Our clients are business owners who want their online presence handled well, without having to manage every detail themselves.

We work with businesses across Europe and worldwide, and you always deal directly with the person responsible for your project.

Why we created our review products

Happy customers rarely leave reviews on their own

Working with local businesses, we kept seeing the same thing. Customers were happy with the service and said so in person, but very few went on to leave a review. Not because they didn't want to, but because finding the right page meant searching for the business, opening the listing and looking for the review button.

Our review cards and table stands remove those steps. A tap or a scan opens the business's own Google review page, while the customer is still there and the experience is fresh.

The goal is simple: make it easier for real customers to share honest feedback. We don't write reviews, we don't offer rewards for them, and we don't promise ratings or rankings.
