Questions

Before your appointment

How much will my property deed cost in total?

The notarial fee is fixed. Stamp duty and registry fees depend on the property value; we send a full written breakdown before the signing date.

Can I sign if I live abroad?

Yes. You can appoint a representative with a power of attorney, which we can prepare for signature at a notary or consulate near you.

Do I need an appointment for certified copies?

Booking is quickest, but short walk-in appointments are available most mornings.

Which languages do you work in?

Appointments are available in English, Italian and French, and we can arrange a sworn translator for other languages.
