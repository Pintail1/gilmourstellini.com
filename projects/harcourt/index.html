The firm

A small firm, by choice

Harcourt & Vale is deliberately small. Every client works directly with an advocate, not a rotating team of juniors, and every file has one person responsible for it.

We advise in English, Italian and Maltese, and many of our consultations happen by video call for clients living abroad.
