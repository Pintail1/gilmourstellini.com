Questions before you book

Do I need a licence to ride a jet ski?

No licence is needed for guided rides. Every rider gets a safety briefing from our dock team before launch.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, a towel, sun cream and your booking confirmation. Life jackets and dry bags are provided.

What happens if the weather turns?

If conditions are unsafe we move your slot or refund you in full.

Can children join?

Children from 8 can ride as passengers with an adult, and the banana boat is suitable from age 6.

Where do we meet?

At our private dock at the north end of Mellieħa Bay, next to the main car park.

Ready to get on the water?

Slots fill fast on weekends — message us on WhatsApp and we'll confirm your slot.
