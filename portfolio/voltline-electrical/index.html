The brief

What the website had to do

People look for an electrician in two moods: planning a job, or dealing with a power cut right now. The site has to reassure the planners and give the emergencies a phone number immediately.

The approach

How the design answers it

A navy and amber palette with a heavy geometric sans reads as dependable. A 24/7 emergency bar with click-to-call sits above the navigation, and a dedicated emergency card shares the hero with the main quote button. Licence and insurance badges, certifications, key numbers and a list of areas served answer the trust questions, and an FAQ accordion handles the rest.

Key features

What's on the site

  • 24/7 emergency bar with click-to-call
  • Emergency callout card in the hero
  • Licensed, insured and 24/7 trust badges
  • Eight service categories
  • Why-us list with key numbers
  • Certifications row and areas served
  • FAQ accordion
  • Quote request call to action
